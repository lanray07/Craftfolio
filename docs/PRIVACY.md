# Privacy disclosure

The public policy is hosted at https://craftfolio-rvs-production.lanraybanks.workers.dev/privacy. Its source is verification-service/privacy.mjs. Contact email banksmi@mail.com was supplied by the developer for public support. This is a factual app disclosure, not a claim of legal certification.

Craftfolio stores imported patterns, projects, notes, materials requirements, counters, undo history and reading positions in private storage on your device. It does not require an account for the local workspace. The application has no advertising or custom analytics and does not upload pattern files or project notes.

Amazon manages purchases and renewals. Craftfolio sends app-specific Amazon user and receipt identifiers to its HTTPS verifier on Cloudflare Workers for Amazon receipt validation. Verified entitlement dates and identifiers are cached locally and bound to the verifier endpoint; test access cannot carry into production. The service has no customer/receipt database, does not intentionally log request payloads or identifiers, and its application logs/traces are disabled. Cloudflare can process operational network metadata under its own policy. Amazon processes purchase information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Imported originals can be opened and exported without Premium, including after a subscription expires. The source file chosen in the system picker is not modified. The app's private copy and project database are removed when Android app data is cleared or Craftfolio is uninstalled. Keep your source files or export originals before uninstalling. This version does not provide project backup/restore or cloud synchronisation.

Core operation requires no microphone, camera, account or broad storage permission. The system file picker grants access to files the user explicitly selects. Internet permission supports purchase verification; local reading and progress updates work without connectivity.

Support and privacy contact: banksmi@mail.com. No cloud account is created by the local workspace, and the verifier has no cloud workspace account to delete. Subscription cancellation is managed through the user's Amazon account; uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.
